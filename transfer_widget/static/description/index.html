Product Transfer Quick View Widget

Business user guide — view product transfer details instantly from Sales, Purchase and Manufacturing lines.

Purpose

The Transfer Widget provides immediate visibility into stock transfers for each product line. Instead of switching to the Inventory app or searching manually, users can click the transfer icon next to a product and see related transfer references, quantities requested, and quantities reserved in a pop-up.

Usage (How to use)

  1. Open a Sales Order, Purchase Order, or Manufacturing Order.
  2. On each product line you will see a small transfer icon next to the delivered/received column.
  3. Click the icon to open a pop-up that shows transfer information for that product: reference, demand quantity, reserved quantity and unit of measure.
  4. Click a reference in the pop-up to open that specific transfer, or click View ALL Transfers to see the full list of related transfers.

No extra navigation or search is required — everything opens from the same screen.

Key Benefits

  • Instant visibility of transfers without leaving the order screen.
  • Fewer clicks — reduces time spent moving between applications.
  • Faster decision-making for Sales, Purchasing and Production teams thanks to immediate access to transfer status.
  • Improved coordination between teams — everyone sees the same up-to-date transfer information.
  • Lower risk of mistakes by showing reserved vs demanded quantities clearly.

Business Flow (what happens)

1. Order created

A Sales / Purchase / Manufacturing order contains product lines.

2. Icon appears

Each product line shows a transfer icon for quick lookup.

3. Click to view

A pop-up shows transfer references, demanded & reserved quantities, and units.

4. Actions

Open a single transfer or view all transfers in a list.

What the pop-up shows

  • Reference: transfer name/number
  • Demand: quantity requested on the move
  • Reserved: quantity reserved on stock move lines
  • Unit: unit of measure
